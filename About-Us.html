About DhartiDhan Organics

DhartiDhan Organics is an Indian manufacturer, supplier, and exporter of premium organic fertilizers and sustainable growing media, with FCO-certified organic fertilizers and sustainable growing media. 
We specialize in producing high-quality Vermicompost, Coco Peat, and Ready-to-Use Potting Mix for agriculture, horticulture, nurseries, landscaping, home gardening, and commercial cultivation.
We proudly serve customers of every size - from home gardeners purchasing small retail packs to farmers, nurseries, commercial growers, distributors, wholesalers, and international importers requiring bulk and container-load quantities.

 Whether you need a single bag for your garden or a long-term supply partner for your business, DhartiDhan Organics delivers reliable, laboratory-tested products with consistent quality and dependable service.

Our Mission

DhartiDhan Organics was founded on a simple belief: healthier soil creates healthier plants, healthier food, and a healthier future. As modern agriculture increasingly depends on synthetic inputs, we saw the need for reliable organic alternatives that help restore soil naturally. Our mission is to manufacture dependable organic growing solutions that support gardeners, farmers, nurseries, and commercial growers while promoting long-term soil health through natural processes.
